Concept

How the level changes

Zoom in on a place until it nearly fills the screen and you're in it. Zoom out and you go back up. Panning moves you across at the level you're already on — it never takes you deeper; zoom or tap for that.

How far a pinch takes you in

Regions: zooming stops at Bordeaux or the Loire; tap to go further. + sub-regions: on to the Médoc or Touraine. All the way: down to the villages.

When it changes

Live: while your fingers move. After a pause: when you slow down for 0.2 s. On release: when you let go.

The gap between the two stops the level flicking back and forth at one zoom.

Inside a region, a small place stays a dot with its name until it's big enough to read as a shape. It only applies where there's a crowd to thin — with this data, South West France. 0 turns it off.

What this prototype tries out
  • The sheet starts as a low bar and only opens when you touch it.
  • Zooming changes the level, as far as the setting above allows. Panning moves you across at the level you're on. Tapping a place glides you in.
  • Around you: the places beside the one you're in, and the other regions whole — never their insides.
  • Inside a place, the smaller places fade in as you zoom toward them.
  • Clearer shades between areas inside a region, in light and dark.
  • Picking a place from the list brings it into view and pulses it.
