# WSET Revision

A personal study app for the WSET Level 2 Award in Wines: study cards by chapter, quizzes, spaced review, timed mocks and drills.
Installable on iPhone (Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen). Works offline.

Progress is stored on the device and synced to a separate **private** repository chosen in Settings; nothing personal is kept in this repo.

The study cards are condensed revision notes, not the textbook itself. Version 1.0.0.
